# AryaOS 2 launch copy

## Launch email

**Subject:** AryaOS 2 is here: one field platform for the TAK sensor edge

**Preheader:** Self-configuring sensors, resilient CoT routing, browser-based
operations, and a complete field lifecycle.

AryaOS 2 is here.

What began as AirTAK, a Raspberry Pi turning local aircraft into TAK tracks, has
become a self-configuring, multi-sensor, fleet-ready edge platform.

AryaOS 1 proved the idea with ADS-B, AIS, Remote ID, and APRS. AOS2 keeps that
offline-first foundation and adds:

- automatic hardware and capability discovery;
- air, maritime, C-UAS, multi-sensor, and relay roles;
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth Remote ID, DroneScout, DJI DroneID, SiK/MAVLink,
  SAPIENT, ACARS, and broader SDR support;
- COTBridge routing to Mesh SA, TAK Server, and additional lanes;
- one-step TAK data-package import and `tak://` enrollment;
- a complete HTTPS field console instead of Node-RED-based administration;
- Tailscale, isolated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth PAN, persistent EMCON, and safe mode;
- one-click updates, support bundles, backup/restore, factory reset, and
  best-effort zeroize; and
- signed packages, SBOMs, mounted-image verification, hardware HIL, and
  lifecycle burn-in gates.

In a disconnected San Diego backpack test, a tuned AryaOS air node received
aircraft as far as 55 miles away. No cloud. No subscription. The box carried
the sensor, the network, and the local picture.

AOS2 extends that operating model across aviation, maritime awareness,
counter-UAS, search and rescue, multi-sensor sites, and CoT relay deployments.

AryaOS 2 `v2.1.19` is available now for Raspberry Pi 3, 4, and 5 arm64 systems.
Moving from v1 requires a fresh flash.

**Flash AOS2:** https://www.aryaos.org/get-started/flash-the-image/

**Build or buy a gateway:** https://www.aryaos.org/get-started/hardware/

**See everything that changed:**
https://www.aryaos.org/launch/aos2/feature-matrix/

AryaOS is open source under Apache 2.0 and developed by Sensors & Signals with
support from the Colorado Center of Excellence for Advanced Technology Aerial
Firefighting and the USDA Forest Service.
